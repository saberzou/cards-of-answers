# Cards of Answers

## Scope
The single-page draw: hold, spread, pick, reveal, keep, and return. Visitor mode: experience.

## Audience and job
A person alone with a private question. They need one answer they can read and keep. The question is never typed.

## Constraints
Static files on GitHub Pages. All 240 answers and 24 illustrations stay. No trackers. Keyboard, zoom, reduced motion, and a screen reader reach the same answer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one paper stage. You hold the closed curtain for a breath, it opens, and twenty-four illustrated cards spring into the opening for you to choose. The chosen card turns and speaks its line. This refuses the dark tarot page, the shuffle button, and a quote card that hides the picture.

OWN-WORLD: Plum auditorium, vermilion proscenium, cream apron, violet curtain, gold footlights. Anybody carries the arch and the stage directions. Petrona speaks the answer. The deck, the arch, and the apron are one object.

STORY: You hold a question you do not type. The lamps mark the breath. You release, choose a picture, and read one sentence. You can keep that card as an image or a link, or ask again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The proscenium fills the screen. "Cards of Answers" is set in the arch. A fanned stack of three illustrated cards stands center stage on the violet curtain. The apron says "Hold your question." with the instruction to press and hold beneath it, then a row of unlit footlights, then "Today's card." No shuffle control.

FORM: Paper toy theater, third grounded candidate, seed e6cb6b9a. The Victorian engraving is translated into the cards' own flat inks. Signature interaction: press-and-hold until the lamps are lit, then release; the curtain parts and the deck springs into a slow orbit. Raises kept from declined challengers: a felt detent when the breath completes, the stage as the only object, the sentence arriving in one motion, the chosen card leaving the company before it opens.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
